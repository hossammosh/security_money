# Platform review
Reviewed 2026-11-02. Country-specific eligibility remains unresolved.

## Test IO — best-verified starting candidate in this review
Official page: https://test.io/company/become-a-tester
Official tester terms: https://test.io/policies-testers

The public page advertises work from anywhere, free registration, onboarding resources, and payment for the first accepted bug or reproduction. It says a PC is required and a mobile device is desirable. It lists PayPal, an IBAN account, or Skrill as payment options and describes monthly payment. Verify the actual onboarding/payment options for the applicant's country; the public page is not a country-specific eligibility guarantee.

Invitations depend on devices, languages, and other factors. Earnings depend on eligible tests, findings, and acceptance. No wage, quick payout, number of paid assignments, or full-time income is promised by this project.

The tester terms say duplicate reports and bugs that cannot be reproduced are not paid; some gigs use lump-sum tasks. Compensation depends on the gig. Section 8 covers broad confidentiality and restricts use of project information beyond providing services. Section 8.1 also covers fee offers and completed-service information. Do not publish project-specific fees or client observations.

This is primarily an entry route into functional QA, not a verified beginner security-consulting job. Accept only authorized tasks; a functional-testing invitation is not authorization for penetration testing, scanning, scraping, or automation.

## HackerOne — later learning-to-bounty route, not salary
Official directory: https://www.hackerone.com/bug-bounty-programs

Rewards depend on individual program rules and qualifying findings. Disclosure-only programs may not pay. This is not guaranteed employment or a dependable beginner income estimate. Verify permitted assets, methods, program eligibility, and payment restrictions. Do not test arbitrary banks or ATMs.
