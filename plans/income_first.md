# Income-first action plan

## Goal and limits
Practice and seek paid remote QA work together, using AI responsibly. Research is optional. No confirmed job, earnings forecast, or payment deadline. Official source review: 2026-11-02.

## Starting candidates
### Test IO
https://test.io/company/become-a-tester
Official page advertises free registration, onboarding resources, remote app testing, accepted-bug/reproduction payments, and monthly payments. Eligibility and supported payout must be checked for the actual country. Assignment invitations and accepted findings are not guaranteed. Prioritize ordinary functional QA; client-specific AI and automation permissions are unverified.

## First seven days — milestones, not an income promise
1. Privately establish country, payout availability, languages, devices/OS, and daily available time. Read actual platform and project rules.
2. Register free on an eligible platform and complete onboarding. Do not pay an intermediary to register or buy equipment based on assumed earnings.
3. Practice one small app/site workflow on an owned or explicitly permitted target. AI can propose a generic checklist; execute checks manually.
4. Write one synthetic/public-safe sample report with real observed evidence from the practice environment. Label it practice, not paid client work.
5. Review invitations if any. Verify from-home completion, scope, allowed tools, compensation and any unreimbursed cost before accepting. Decline unsuitable tasks.
6. Conduct an accepted task exactly as authorized, reproduce findings, check scope/duplicates where allowed, and submit manually verified reports. If no invitations exist, continue onboarding/practice rather than claiming earnings.
7. Review actual outcomes privately and adjust. For client-specific fees and project statistics, obey confidentiality terms; never publish them here.

## First useful deliverable
A repeatable test checklist and a clear bug report for an authorized practice target. Do not invent a bug or claim completion before testing.

## Progress measures, private only where sensitive
Track eligible invitations, accepted assignments, accepted/rejected reports, time spent, actual receipts, and permitted expense records. Separate accepted earnings from money received. Include AI fees and setup time when assessing effective hourly return. Platform rules may restrict disclosure of compensation and other project information.

## Later route, not today's prerequisite
After practical experience, consider narrowly scoped freelance QA with explicit client authorization for AI use. Security assessments require additional competence and permission. Bug bounties are not a guaranteed beginner income plan.
