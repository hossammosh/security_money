# Four-week income-first checkpoints

Updated 2026-11-02. This supersedes the earlier mandatory research schedule. Research has no required time allocation. No guaranteed tasks or earnings.

## Week 1: become eligible and practice
Verify country/payment eligibility and terms, complete free onboarding, create one public-safe practice checklist/report. Use AI on generic or synthetic content only unless explicit applicable permissions allow more.

## Week 2: seek authorized tasks
Review invitations if available and accept suitable from-home tasks. Reproduce findings, follow project reporting rules, and learn from feedback. If invitations are unavailable, continue practice and assess another eligible platform. No fabricated completed work.

## Week 3: improve execution
Focus on clear reporting, scope compliance, device troubleshooting and efficient verification. Use AI where permitted; no unauthorized client uploads or automated scanning. Track outcomes privately without publishing confidential fees or project information.

## Week 4: evaluate actual return
Assess actual eligibility, available assignments, accepted findings, money received, total time and allowed expenses. Continue worthwhile routes, adjust ineffective ones, and avoid spending based on hypothetical income. Research remains optional. Do not market yourself as an expert security consultant before demonstrating the relevant skills.
