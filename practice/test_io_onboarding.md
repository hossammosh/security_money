# Test IO onboarding: practical first steps

Guide updated 2026-11-02 from observed dashboard controls. This is a general guide, not a public account-status log. No account screenshot or personal details are stored here. Interface labels can change.

## Immediate sequence
1. Open **Your profile** and add languages under the relevant profile section. List only languages you can genuinely test/report in. The dashboard shows **Add language** as mandatory.
2. Open **Your Devices** and add equipment you actually own and can use, with the correct model, OS/version and supported browsers where requested. The dashboard shows **Add devices** as mandatory. Do not buy new devices just to qualify for hypothetical future work.
3. Click **Go to learning portal**. The observed interface uses a six-module mandatory-onboarding progress indicator. Complete the modules shown in the account; their individual names were not visible in the dashboard. Do not assume all are paid or that completing them guarantees invitations.
4. Complete the **Exploratory Testing Course**, also listed among test-cycle unlock criteria. Its relationship to the six-module indicator is not established by the dashboard alone; follow actual portal instructions and avoid assuming it is a separate seventh module.
5. Return to **Dashboard** and check whether **Available Test Cycles** has unlocked. Complete any remaining criteria actually displayed. Unlocking is not a guarantee that a paid task is immediately available.

## Learning method
Read each lesson, write a short explanation in your own words, try only its authorized practice exercise, then complete required checks yourself. AI can explain generic terminology and generate independent practice examples. Do not share assessment questions if restricted, outsource graded onboarding, or assume permission to submit AI-written answers. No confidential course/client material goes to public GitHub or external AI without permission.

## First assignment checklist
Before accepting: verify scope, test window, allowed devices, known issues/duplicate handling, report requirements, compensation, allowed tools and remote-only feasibility. Decline projects requiring location visits, real payment spending, or unavailable devices unless knowingly and appropriately accepted. Functional QA scope does not authorize security scanning or attacks.

Reproduce a suspected issue, confirm it is not expected behavior or a known duplicate where permitted, capture authorized evidence, and submit factual observations. Use the project's severity rules. Do not fabricate bugs, steps, or screenshots.

## Safe help requests
Describe a confusing concept in your own words without client identifiers, private URLs, account details, or confidential screenshots. For navigation help, provide a screenshot only where permitted and redact personal information. Never share passwords, OTPs, identity documents or banking details.

## Payment
Use **Billing** for legitimate payout setup when needed, entering sensitive information directly on the official platform. Actual country/payment eligibility remains to be verified. Registration is free and no task or income is guaranteed.
